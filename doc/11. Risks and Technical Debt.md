## Manipulation

| Priority | Title | Risk Description | Measures (simplified) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **P1 (High)** | Grasping objects with uneven weight distribution or complex shapes | Off-center center of mass / irregular shape can cause tilting or slipping during lift. | • Grip as close as possible to the geometric center; prefer top-down when possible.<br>• Lift slowly, pause briefly to stabilize, then move.<br>• Use high-friction finger pads and sufficient grip force.<br>• If tilting is observed, set down and use a predefined alternate grasp. |
| **P1 (High)** | Collision avoidance in tight environments | Small clearances can lead to touching or knocking over nearby items. | • Slow down significantly near the target.<br>• Maintain a fixed clearance (e.g., ≥ 2 cm) and prefer top-down approaches.<br>• Stop briefly before placing, check surroundings, then lower. |
| **P1 (High)** | Precise determination of grasp points | Inaccurate grasp points reduce success rate and increase retries. | • Default to the center of the visible surface; prefer top-down.<br>• If uncertain, take a short second view (slightly offset) before grasping.<br>• For known parts, use a simple list of predefined grasp points. |
| **P1 (High)** | Precise determination of placement points | Misplaced targets cause misalignment, overhangs, or insertion errors. | • Align to a clear reference (mark/edge/stop).<br>• Lower very slowly in the last ~5 cm; release only when stable.<br>• After release, do a quick visual check; if offset, correct or re-place. |

## Movement

- Different surfaces (carpet, Wood, PVC, marble, …)
- Differentiating between Human and object (different distance to be kept)
- Energy consumption: movement should be efficient so that enough power remains for manipulation and other computing
- Localization drift: wheel slip, uneven flooring or colission can cause odometry errors
- compute headroom consideration: SLAM, perception (LIDAR) and pathplanning may exceed computing capacity (tho not yet tested, since the hardware wasnt fully available at the time)
- Poor simulation-to-reality transfer: until now movement was only tested in simulation. transferrence to real hardware might bring up a different set of problems then anticipated

## Safety

- **Real-time capability:** Communication bottlenecks may occur during initial runtime tests.
- **Resuming processes after system-wide interventions:** Ensuring flawless continuation of processes after global error handling actions (e.g. a system freeze) remains a challenge.
- **Data load from communication:** High data traffic caused by heartbeat messages from all critical nodes to the local safety nodes, and from the local safety nodes to the central safety node.
