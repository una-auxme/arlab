- Driving Forces

  - Participation in RoboCup @Home
    The primary goal of the project is to develop a robot capable of competing in the RoboCup @Home league, which defines many of the functional and non-functional requirements.

  - Exploration of current state-of-the-art
    Evaluate and experiment with the latest technologies in robotics relevant to the @Home category (e.g., perception, navigation, interaction) in order to understand strengths, weaknesses, and applicability for our system.

  - Building a sustainable foundation
    Establish a solid basis in software and hardware architecture, enabling future student groups to continue development and improve the system incrementally.

  - Industry cooperation
    Collaborate with hardware and software companies to strengthen partnerships, gain practical insights, and integrate state-of-the-art solutions into the project.

| Goal                            | Motivation                                                                                                       |
| ------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Extensibility & Modularity**  | System must be easy to extend and adapt by future student groups; components should be reusable across projects. |
| **Safety**                      | Guarantee safe interaction between humans and the robot, and protect the system from damage.                     |
| **Robustness**                  | Reliable operation in dynamic, noisy, and unpredictable real-world environments.                                 |
| **Real-time performance**       | Timely and predictable responses in perception, planning, and control loops.                                     |
| **Maintainability & Usability** | Code and architecture must be understandable, easy to evolve, and simple for new students/developers to use.     |

## Out of Scope

    The project is not intended as a commercial product, but as a research and learning platform. Focus is on the RoboCup @Home context.
