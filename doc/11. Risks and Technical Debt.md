## Movement

- Different surfaces (carpet, Wood, PVC, marble, …)
- Differentiating between Human and object (different distance to be kept)
- Energy consumption: movement should be efficient so that enough power remains for manipulation and other computing
- Localization drift: wheel slip, uneven flooring or colission can cause odometry errors
- compute headroom consideration: SLAM, perception (LIDAR) and pathplanning may exceed computing capacity (tho not yet tested, since the hardware wasnt fully available at the time)
- Poor simulation-to-reality transfer: until now movement was only tested in simulation. transferrence to real hardware might bring up a different set of problems then anticipated

## Safety

- **Real-time capability:** Communication bottlenecks may occur during initial runtime tests.
- **Resuming processes after system-wide interventions:** Ensuring flawless continuation of processes after global error handling actions (e.g. a system freeze) remains a challenge.
- **Data load from communication:** High data traffic caused by heartbeat messages from all critical nodes to the local safety nodes, and from the local safety nodes to the central safety node.
