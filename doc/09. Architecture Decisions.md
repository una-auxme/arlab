Safety:

Decision Making for a Hybrid Architecture of Decentralized Safety Submodules and a Central Safety Node

- **Scalability & Team Efficiency**
  - *Problem:* We cannot afford a dedicated safety team, and external enforcement would slow down development.
  - *Solution:* By enabling each team to implement safety locally by serving templates, we integrate safety directly into the development process without extra overhead.

- **Simplicity & Adaptability**
  - *Problem:* Centralized safety logic would become complex and hard to adapt as features evolve.
  - *Solution:* Local safety submodules with simple templates keep safety close to the local functionality and local paramaters. This allows quick adjustments.

- **Error Transparency & Consistent Decisions**
  - *Problem:* Without structured aggregation, local errors remain hidden and handling becomes inconsistent.
  - *Solution:* The central safety node collects all submodule states, evaluates severity, and ensures uniform reactions across the system.

- **Reliability & Compliance**
  - *Problem:* Failures in local modules could compromise overall safety and make audits difficult.
  - *Solution:* Central reporting ensures robustness, provides reliable monitoring, and creates a single point of truth for audits and certification.
 
- Data Accessibility
-   Titel: Making Relevant Sensor Data Accessible from All System Components
-   Context: We manage multiple system components, each with varying sensor data needs at different times. Our approach ensures that each component can access the specific data it requires, when it’s needed, minimizing redundancy and optimizing performance across the system.
-   Decision:We are building a data pipeline that includes perception and computer vision modules, processing and interpreting visual data from multiple sensors. Using this pipeline enables scalability with sensors, specific processing at multiple stages, simple implementation, and a clean separation of context.
-   Status: accepted
-   Consequences: Data is only accessible at defined stages.
