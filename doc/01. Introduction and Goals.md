Manipulation Description:
The goal is the integration of a manipulator. It should be capable of grasping, transporting, and placing objects. Objectives include secure grasping and placement without losing objects, effective path planning, and reaching the pose currently required for the task.

Manipulation: 
- Reliability – manipulator must perform grasping, transporting & placing tasks without dropping or damaging objects
- Precision – Accurate positioning and path planning
- Robustness – The system should handle a wide range of object types
- Modularity – manipulatior should be easily maintainable, replaceable & integrable with other components (e.g. new gripper)
- Efficiency – Movements and planning should be optimized for time & energy without compromising safety or accuracy
